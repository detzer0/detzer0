
## About me

Hi there! My name is Denis, and I am a postdoctoral researcher in applied mathematics at the [Weierstrass Institute for Applied Analysis and Stochastics (WIAS)](https://www.wias-berlin.de/) in Berlin.



My current research focuses on the structure-aware second-order training of neural PDE solvers (PINNs, deep operator networks, hybrid solvers). More broadly, I like to design structure-aware numerical algorithms for problems in computational engineering. I am particularly interested in approaches that connect mathematical modeling and variational analysis in function spaces with suitable discretization strategies and efficient numerical solvers. Methodologically, I view this as an iterative process in which insights from the infinite-dimensional problem guide discretization and solver design, while computational considerations and practical needs, in turn, inform the mathematical modeling and formulation.



My work spans numerical analysis, PDE-constrained optimization, reduced-order modeling, and physics-informed machine learning. My interests include adjoint-based methods, domain decomposition, and low-rank approximation techniques for efficient numerical optimization in physics-informed machine learning.
